HACKATHON PS-29 · MEETING-TO-TASK DELEGATOR

Meetings don't fail on decisions.They fail on vague promises.

Ergon turns meeting transcripts into reviewable commitments: it surfaces vague language, shows what's missing, suggests clearer SMART tasks, and helps your team follow through.

01 / THE PROMISEFrom conversation to accountable work

01 THE PROBLEM

A meeting can end
with nothing owned.

Hour-long conversations can leave behind an unclear promise—and no shared next step.

01Everyone nodded.Nothing happened.

02“I'll look into it.”A promise without edges.

03No owner.No deadline.No ticket.

When ownership, timing, or a deliverable is left unsaid, teams are left to reconstruct the commitment after the call.

02 THE WORKFLOW

From messy transcript
to tracked task.

Three deliberate steps. Suggestions stay suggestions until a person reviews and confirms them.

  1. 01

    Paste

    Bring meeting notes or a supported transcript export. Ergon reads the text for possible promises and concrete follow-up.

    .txt · .vtt · .srt
  2. 02

    Review

    Inspect the quote, the clarity signals, the missing owner, deadline, or deliverable—and edit the suggested SMART task.

    Evidence stays visible
  3. 03

    Accept

    Confirm only the work your team is ready to own. Confirmed tasks move to the Board and appear in the Dashboard.

    Human confirmation required
Review → clarify → confirm

03 THE WORKSPACE

One promise.
Three ways forward.

Start with the source. Keep every decision reviewable, then carry confirmed work forward.

03.1 REVIEW

Find the promise
behind the words.

Read the transcript beside the suggestions. Ergon distinguishes possible commitments from general discussion and points to the source wording behind each one.

  • See the exact transcript evidence.
  • Spot missing owners, deadlines, and deliverables.
  • Edit suggested tasks and their details before confirming.

The score is inspectable. The commitment is still yours to confirm.

03.2 BOARD

Turn commitments
into work.

The Board keeps approved tasks together for the current browser session, with the details a teammate needs to act and a simple view of where the work stands.

  • Move tasks across To Do, Doing, and Done.
  • Review owners, deadlines, and deliverables.
  • Update task details as plans become clearer.

External ticket creation is not connected in this frontend preview. Jira and Slack are future directions, not current integrations.

03.3 DASHBOARD

Make Meeting
Debt visible.

Meeting Debt is a workflow heuristic calculated from unresolved promises in the current review session—not a forecast or a performance metric.

  • Count identified commitments and clarity bands.
  • Find open tasks without an owner or deadline.
  • See approved work move through its statuses.

This repository is a browser-only preview. It has no shared or durable task store.

04 THE CLARITY MODEL

A transparent score,
not a black box.

Four explicit rules estimate how much clarification a potential commitment needs. Each contributes a named number of vagueness points.

Owner missing
+30
Deadline missing
+30
Deliverable missing
+20
Hedge words
+20
0–15 Clear16–45 Fuzzy46–100 Vague

These are the scoring bands used by the repository's current local rules preview. A score flags details to review; it does not prove that a firm commitment was made.

05 DATA & PRIVACY

Clear about what
happens to text.

A factual account of this repository's frontend preview.

01 / ANALYSIS

Local rules only

This frontend preview runs a small rules-based analysis in the browser. It does not call an AI service or API. Transcript text stays in the current browser session; the example transcript is sample content.

02 / REDACTION

No built-in masking

The current preview has no redaction control. Remove sensitive details yourself before entering a transcript, even though this version does not upload it to an analysis API.

03 / STORAGE

Session, not sync

No account, shared team store, or durable task database is connected. Do not rely on the browser preview as a long-term record or an offline-first application.

06 HELP

A few useful
details.

Know what the current browser preview does before you bring a transcript in.

Open Ergon ↗
What can I upload? +

The current page accepts .txt, .vtt, and .srt transcript files, or pasted text. Speaker names help the local rules preview anchor possible commitments.

Does Ergon create tasks automatically? +

No. Review the suggestions and approve only the work you are ready to own. Approved tasks move to the Board in the current browser session.

Does this version use AI or sync teams? +

No. This repository is a frontend-only preview. It uses local rules, has no AI/API integration, and does not sync team records.
