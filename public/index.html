HELP CENTER

How do I start a review?

Open Review, paste meeting text with speaker names or choose a .txt, .vtt, or .srt file, then select Review commitments. The file is read locally in this browser.

What does the vague score mean?

It is a transparent SMART-gap heuristic. It adds points when owner, deadline, or deliverable is missing or when hedge language appears. It is not a prediction of performance.

Does this version use AI or sync teams?

No. This frontend preview uses a small local rules preview and the provided example. It does not call an AI service, store shared records, or connect team memberships.

How do reminders work?

Approve a task and enter a full YYYY-MM-DD deadline to get a Google Calendar draft link. Reminder text can also be copied; nothing is sent automatically.

Where can I view tasks?

Approved promises appear on the Board. Use the move buttons to update To Do, Doing, and Done. The Dashboard summarizes the current browser session.
